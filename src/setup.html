Sandbox MCP · Local setup

Your ideas. A playable sandbox.

Connect your AI app to Garry's Mod. Ask it to build a scene, inspect the result, and improve it with you.

1. Choose your AI app

Setup adds Sandbox MCP to the app you choose and keeps a backup of its existing configuration.

2. Connect in Garry's Mod

Install the Sandbox MCP Workshop addon, then start a Sandbox map.

Open Q → Utilities → Sandbox MCP → Connection, review the screenshot option and click Connect AI.

Checking the game…

3. Make something together

Restart your AI app after setup, enable its Sandbox MCP tools, then try:

Build a small drivable cargo rover near me. Find suitable props, keep the driver's view clear, weld the cargo to the vehicle, and take a screenshot to check your work.

Your AI can inspect the world and change the objects it creates. You can undo individual builds or edits. Disconnect in-game to stop access. Your AI app supplies the model; no API key is stored in this addon.

Other MCP clients and manual setup

Use a client that supports local stdio MCP servers. Copy the entry below into its configuration. Browser-only chat apps may not support local servers.

The companion is installed under your local application data when you connect an app. For a manual-only setup, keep this executable in a permanent folder and use sandbox-mcp config to get its exact path.
This page runs on your computer. Setup needs no account, administrator rights, Node.js or Python. The game addon is distributed separately through Workshop.

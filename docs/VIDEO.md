# Launch showcase

[Watch the 26-second video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=30DsajKY70I).

Native 1920x1080, 30 fps gameplay captured in Anode. Cutbolt assembled four shots,
rendered captions, placed an original synthesized electronic score and exported
H.264/AAC stereo. No game audio was captured. A running Wiremod pulse display,
batched sculpture creation, cargo rover and physics/undo all use public MCP tools.
Private camera/capture helpers are excluded from the GMA.

Editable Cutbolt sessions, masters, plans and receipts are retained locally in
artifacts/video. The full export decodes successfully. Title and undo beats were
visually inspected. The video is unlisted on YouTube and linked from Workshop,
matching the existing mod-showcase approach.
